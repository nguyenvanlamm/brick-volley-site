Support

Questions, a bug or a level that feels unfair: email lamnv260398@gmail.com. Please say which phone you play on and which level you were in.

How the paddle works

After you release a shot, touch and hold anywhere on the board. A paddle rises under your finger, just below the floor line. Slide it under the falling balls: each ball it meets goes back up once per turn. A ball that meets the centre goes straight up; the edges send it wide. Lift your finger to lower the paddle.

Restoring the full game

On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the unlock sheet or About and tap Restore purchases. The store checks the account you are signed in with; the app never sees your name, email or payment details.

Progress

Your progress saves on the device after every turn. It is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.
