Privacy Policy

Brick Volley collects no personal data. Your progress is saved only on this device and is never sent anywhere. There are no ads, accounts or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

To let you continue where you left off, the app keeps a few small values in your device's local storage: your game in progress, the highest level you have unlocked, your best score per level, your sound setting and whether you have bought the full game. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchase

Levels 1–4 are free; the full game (Level 5 onward) is a one-time purchase. The payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase exists — never your name, email address or payment details. To restore a purchase on a new device the app asks the store, which uses the account you are signed in with.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared.

Children

Brick Volley is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

This page uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. The host serving it may keep standard server logs.
